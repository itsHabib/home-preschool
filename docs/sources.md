# Sources

Every fact printed on a play page, and every book or song in a week's basket, was
checked against the sources below. This is a source ledger, not a claim about what
any child has learned.

## Dinosaur facts (Unit 1)

Child pages use the short claims only. Time periods are parent vocabulary, not
recall targets. All art is stylized; its color and exact plate counts are not
fossil claims.

| Dinosaur | Printed claims | Source 1 | Source 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stegosaurus | Ate plants; Late Jurassic; back plates and tail spikes | [Natural History Museum directory](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory/stegosaurus.html) | [Utah State University Prehistoric Museum fact sheet](https://eastern.usu.edu/prehistoric-museum/_files/exhibits/handout-stegosaurus.pdf) |
| Triceratops | Ate plants; Late Cretaceous; three horns and a frill | [Natural History Museum directory](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory/triceratops.html) | [AMNH OLogy card](https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/ology-cards/010-triceratops-horridus?view=modal), with the frill confirmed by the [AMNH specimen description](https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/permanent/ornithischian-dinosaurs/triceratops) |
| Ankylosaurus | Ate plants; Late Cretaceous; bony armor and a tail club | [Natural History Museum directory](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory/ankylosaurus.html) | [Arbour and Mallon 2017, FACETS 2:764–794](https://dinodata.de/bibliothek/pdf_u/2017/01_facets-2017-0063_dd.pdf) |
| Tyrannosaurus rex (contrast only) | Ate meat; Late Cretaceous; large head, small two-fingered arms | [Natural History Museum directory](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory/tyrannosaurus.html) | [AMNH](https://www.amnh.org/dinosaurs/tyrannosaurus-rex) |

The kit prints only the agreed period names, never numerical dates. It makes no
claims about dinosaur colors, herd nesting, sounds, intelligence, or the purpose
of Stegosaurus plates. The egg is a generic egg, the leaf is a symbol for plants,
and the meat is a pretend-food pictogram. "Plant-eaters with armor" is a theme,
not a taxonomic family, and no scene claims these animals shared a time or place.

## Books and songs (Unit 1)

These are choices to borrow or use if you already have them; one book or one song
is enough. No lyrics or passages are reproduced. Sound links are spoken play by
the adult, not printed-word decoding.

- [Dinosaur Roar! — Paul and Henrietta Stickland](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Dinosaur-Roar%21/Paul-Stickland/Dinosaur-Roar%21/9781665959414)
- [How Do Dinosaurs Say Good Night? — Jane Yolen; illustrated by Mark Teague](https://clubs.scholastic.com/how-do-dinosaurs-say-good-night%3F/9780590316828-rco-us.html): Week 1 uses the spoken word "say" for /s/.
- [Ten Little Dinosaurs — Mike Brownlow; illustrated by Simon Rickerty](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/mike-brownlow/ten-little-dinosaurs/9781408344040/): Week 2 uses spoken "ten" for /t/, without requiring a count to ten.
- [Apples and Pumpkins — Anne Rockwell; illustrated by Lizzy Rockwell](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Apples-and-Pumpkins/Anne-Rockwell/9781442476561): Week 3 uses spoken "apple" for /a/.
- [We Are the Dinosaurs — Laurie Berkner](https://laurieberkner.com/song-lyrics/we-are-the-dinosaurs-2): a movement favorite, all weeks.
- [Stomp Like a Dinosaur — Super Simple Songs](https://supersimple.com/song/stomp-like-a-dinosaur/): /s/ in spoken "stomp", Week 1.
- [Five Enormous Dinosaurs — traditional action rhyme](https://goulburn-p.schools.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/doe/sws/schools/g/goulburn-p/pdfs/playgroup/2020/PlaygroupsAtHomeBooklet05.pdf): a countdown to stomp to, all weeks; no subtraction expected.
- [Twinkle Twinkle Little Star — Super Simple Songs](https://supersimple.com/song/twinkle-twinkle-little-star/): /t/ in spoken "twinkle", Week 2.
- [Apples & Bananas — Super Simple Songs](https://supersimple.com/song/apples-and-bananas/): Week 3 uses the ordinary first verse for short /a/; the vowel-swapping verses are silly play, not the sound model.

## Art

The dinosaur, egg, leaf and meat drawings were generated with an image model as
black-and-white coloring-book line art, checked by a person for the defining
features (plates and tail spikes, three horns and a frill, armor and a club tail),
then traced to editable SVG. `art/README.md` records the prompts and the
per-drawing checks. The small props (sun, cup, sock, ball, towel, apple) are
hand-drawn SVG pictograms.
