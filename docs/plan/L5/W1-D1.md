# W1-D1 Meet Stegosaurus
Parent: W1 — introduce /s/ and printed S while inviting dough loops and free circles.
Serves: R1 R2 M0
Goal: Enjoy Stegosaurus coloring, an oral sound choice, and round play through one chosen 2–10-minute route.
  Template: coloring-page; portrait US Letter with the child's name at the top, one large complete Stegosaurus line drawing, an isolated S, and a short fact strip: “Stegosaurus ate plants. It had back plates and tail spikes.” Leave most of the sheet for coloring; no sound-choice pictures or tracing guides. In a compact adult footer, name the sound decision and harder rung; repeat both on the separate parent card. Free circles go on scrap paper, not a preprinted circle target.
  Decision the child makes: Which spoken word starts with /s/: sun or cup?
  Invitation (two alternatives): “Want to color Stegosaurus with me, or roll a dough loop beside it?” Both are core ways to start; either can include the brief oral sound game, and both may be declined.
  Setup/play: The coloring page, crayons and scrap paper; a little dough on a washable mat. Share the dinosaur strip as you play. Let them color or join the ends of a dough snake. A free crayon circle on scrap is welcome too. For a sound turn, say “sun” and “cup” naturally; ask which starts with /s/. If the words or game are new, show one together first. Point to S and model /s/; an echo is welcome. If they want a repeat, reverse the spoken word order.
  Harder rung: If that sound game is easy, try a fresh spoken pair: ball or sock.
  Repeat play: Reverse the spoken word order on a willing repeat; speak both words naturally. Printed sound choices vary sides across weeks.
  Materials: One activity page, one parent card, crayons, playdough, washable mat, and blank scrap paper. No toys, picture cards, or cutting are needed; a parent stays present.
Exit check: During at most two minutes of willing play, notice either the sound choice or a free crayon mark. The card asks one question: “What did the child choose or make today?” A word, gesture, dough loop, open crayon curve, closed rough crayon circle, or “not today” is enough to note. Distinguish dough/finger practice from an independently closed crayon circle; no completion or repeat test is required.
Children:
Constraints inherited:
- Calibration: counts 1–4 with meaning; does not recognize letters, including their own name; cannot yet draw a circle; gets frustrated when forced.
- Every activity lasts 2–10 minutes, hands first; no pencils, sitting lessons or draw-the-line tasks before M1. Per the parent level check, playdough, magnet tiles, Lego, crayons, cut cards, clips and rings are fine with a parent present.
- Set it out and play near it. Every card offers two invitations; the child may decline both. Stop immediately on pushback. Never move their hand, supply the answer, or require finishing.
- One printable page and one parent card each day. Name the actual learning decision and a conditional harder rung on both. An invitation choice alone is not the learning decision.
- Numbers begin at 3–4; stretch to 5 only when ready. Numerals ride along without required recognition. Subitizing remains unknown.
- Sounds, not letter names; U1 introduces only s/t/a. No CVC tile reading or tricky words. The child's name is on every page for optional finger exploration; no name-letter testing or required letter formation.
- Diet, era and feature claims need two sources. Stegosaurus is the star; the three U1 dinosaurs and T. rex may supply contrasts. No invented colors, plate purpose, or claim these dinosaurs lived together.
- M0 opportunities include dough loops and free crayon circles on scrap paper. Dough and finger play are practice; only an independent rough crayon circle meets M0.
- Exit checks are optional two-minute glimpses during play. Parent cards ask one plain question about what happened; declining is fine and never failure.
Open questions:
- ASSUMED: first-sound readiness is unknown; start with the two familiar spoken words sun/cup, treat no response warmly, and adjust after parent observation. Facts and art need the parent build pass before rendering.
