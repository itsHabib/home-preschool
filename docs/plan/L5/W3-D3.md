# W3-D3 Four pretend leaves for Ankylosaurus
Parent: W3 — Give W3 a plant-food choice and a meaningful stop at four from eight.
Serves: N1 L1
Goal: Invite the child to choose a pretend meal for Ankylosaurus and serve four leaves.
- Template: feed-the-dino; one portrait US Letter page with a horizontal activity area containing Ankylosaurus beside a large empty meal space. Place leaf and meat food pictures below as equally sized choices, without checked answers or category colors. Include an incidental 4 and the child's name in the footer; no four-slot plate or already-served leaves. The parent card and adult-facing footer repeat the decision, invitations, extension and observation.
- Decision the child makes: Choose plants or meat for Ankylosaurus, then decide when their served set contains four leaves and stop taking from the supply.
- Invitation (two alternatives): "Shall we make its pretend leaves together, or shall I make leaves while you choose its food?" the child can decline both.
- Setup/play: 2–10 minutes with a parent present. Set the page next to dough and a mat. Make eight pretend dough leaves by flattening eight pieces into simple leaf shapes; parent can prepare them if the child prefers choosing and serving. Name the two food pictures neutrally. During teaching, say that Ankylosaurus ate plants, then chat and play; do not treat an immediate repeat as independent recall. On a fresh willing turn offer both food pictures and let them choose, then ask for four leaves from the unsorted pile of eight. The child moves dough leaves into the empty meal space and stops themself; parent neither counts along nor sets four apart. There is no need to make realistic leaves or finish the meal. A picture point, word or pretend feeding action counts as a response.
- Harder rung: If they readily serves four, clear the meal space, return all eight leaves to the supply and invite a five-leaf meal on a later willing turn.
- Materials: One intact printable page with both food pictures, one parent card, enough playdough for eight pretend leaves and a dough mat. Parent stays present; no real food, extra printed sheets or dinosaur toy are required.
Exit check: Optional, within two minutes during pretend feeding: the parent card asks "What food did the child choose, and how many pieces did they serve?" On a willing fresh turn offer both food pictures and request four from the eight leaves without repeating the diet answer. Notice food choice and the final quantity separately; help or declining is fine. One dinosaur response advances L1 without claiming the eight-dinosaur milestone met.
Children:
Constraints inherited:
- Starting calibration, from the first level check: "Counting things | 1–4 with meaning"; "Letters | Does not recognise letters yet, including their own name"; "Drawing | Cannot draw a circle yet"; "Temperament | Frustrated when they feel forced".
- Numbers start with 3–4 objects, stretch to 5 only when ready; dot-to-dot matching starts at 3 then 4. Numerals ride along from W1, never required answers. Subitizing and play checks A, B, D, G, I remain unknown.
- Every activity lasts 2–10 minutes, hands first; no pencils, sitting lessons or draw-the-line tasks before M1. Per the parent level check, playdough, magnet tiles, Lego, crayons, cut cards, clips and rings are fine with a parent present.
- Set it out and play near it. Every card offers two invitations; the child may decline both. Stop immediately on pushback. Never move their hand, supply the answer, or require finishing.
- One printable page and one parent card each day. Name the actual learning decision and a conditional harder rung on both. An invitation choice alone is not the learning decision.
- Synthetic phonics uses sounds, not letter names: initial set s a t p i n; U1's specified dinosaur order is S, T, A. No other taught graphemes yet. Oral blending comes before letter-tile words; no CVC tile reading or tricky words in U1. Tricky words start at R4, two at a time.
- One focal dinosaur per week; its initial is the week's sound. All pages carry the child's name for optional finger exploration, without name-letter testing. No letter formation requirement.
- Diet, era and feature claims need two sources. The weekly dinosaur is the star; the three U1 dinosaurs and T. rex may supply contrasts. No invented colors, plate purpose, or claim these dinosaurs lived together.
- M0 opportunities include dough loops and free crayon circles on scrap paper. Dough and finger play are practice; only an independent rough crayon circle meets M0.
- Exit checks are optional two-minute glimpses during play. Parent cards ask one plain question about what happened; declining is fine and never failure.
- Focal dinosaur and sound: Ankylosaurus; A /a/ as in apple and ankle. Facts to verify before build: Bony armor and a tail club; Late Cretaceous; plants.
